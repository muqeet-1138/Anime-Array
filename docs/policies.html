Policies

Content, copyright and intellectual property

Anime Array is a fan project built on other people's characters. That creates an obligation to be precise about whose work it is, what has been done with it, and what will be removed on request. This page is that precision.

Summary

Anime Array is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, licensed by or sponsored by any anime studio, publisher, licensor, broadcaster or rights holder. It hosts no video and reproduces no protected media. It does not clone voices. Copyright concerns are handled through the contact form and are prioritised above all other enquiries.

Non-affiliation

Anime Array is not affiliated with, connected to, endorsed by, sponsored by, licensed by, authorised by or approved by any anime studio, manga publisher, licensor, broadcaster, distributor, streaming service or rights holder. No such party has reviewed, approved or been consulted about this project.

This is stated plainly because a fan project that lets its association with a studio go ambiguous causes real problems for real rights holders, and because claiming an endorsement that does not exist is both a lie and, in most jurisdictions, actionable. Anime Array does not make that claim and does not permit anybody else to make it on its behalf.

Ownership of names and likenesses

All anime and manga series titles, character names, designs, catchphrases and related intellectual property belong to their respective owners. Anime Array uses these names for one narrow purpose: identifying which existing character each original AI conversation system is inspired by, so that readers can find the character they are looking for.

Naming a work in order to identify it is different from reproducing it. On Anime Array:

  • No animation is shown. No video, episode, film or clip is hosted, uploaded, embedded or mirrored.
  • No manga is shown. No panel, page or illustration is reproduced.
  • No audio is shown. No voice performance, soundtrack or subtitle track is hosted or reproduced.
  • No script is reproduced. Character replies are original text generated in a character's voice, not quotations.
  • No link to unauthorised downloads is provided anywhere on the platform.

The characters on Anime Array are original conversational systems inspired by existing anime characters. Each has been given its own written personality and set of behaviours. None is a copy of any performance, script or protected text.

Character replies are not canon

It is worth being precise about this, because it is the point most likely to be misunderstood. A character reply from Anime Array is an original conversational response generated in a character's voice. It is not:

  • a quotation from an anime script, dub, light novel or manga panel;
  • a statement of a factual claim made by the rights holder;
  • an official continuation of a series;
  • the opinion of the character as expressed by their creator.

Where a character is given a situation the source material never covers — which happens constantly, because the point is that you can ask them anything — the reply is reasoned from that character's temperament and stated as an original response. Treating such a reply as a factual claim about the source material is a misreading of what the platform is.

No voice cloning

Anime Array does not use voice cloning, voice conversion or any technique that reproduces a real person's vocal performance.

No character's dialogue is spoken by a real person in real time. No voice actor's performance is reproduced, sampled or imitated on the platform. The characters are text-based conversational systems only, and no audio synthesis of any performer is offered. Voice actors have not participated in, authorised or endorsed this project.

Generated images

Images produced through Vision AI are original outputs generated by the platform. They are not reproductions of any existing animation, manga panel, photograph, artwork or other protected work, and they are not derived from any copyrighted source image.

Requests that attempt to reproduce protected material, to recreate an identifiable real person, or to generate content that is illegal or exploitative are refused. The platform's Terms of Service set out the terms under which generated output may be used, and anyone intending to use generated images commercially should read them first rather than assuming.

Acceptable use

The platform is free and open, but it is not a free-for-all. Behaviour that is not acceptable includes:

  • Using the platform to harass, threaten or abuse anyone, in character or out of it.
  • Submitting prompts designed to elicit sexual content involving minors, or any content exploiting a real person.
  • Attempting to circumvent rate limits, access controls or the limits of any free tier in order to gain unfair advantage.
  • Automated scraping of the platform at a rate that degrades it for other people.
  • Republishing large portions of the platform's documentation or design under a licence that does not permit it.
  • Presenting generated output as a real quote, a real person's view, or an official statement.

Character replies that are distasteful because a character is written as they are — Levi being terse, Vegeta being proud, Sukuna being dismissive — are the intended behaviour of the product and are not a violation. The distinction being drawn is between a character staying in character and someone using the platform to cause harm.

Takedown and correction requests

Rights holders and concerned parties can use the contact form or email contact@animearray.site. Requests are prioritised above all other correspondence, including feature requests.

To make a request actionable, include:

  1. the specific work you are contacting Anime Array about;
  2. the URL on Anime Array concerned;
  3. what you would like removed, changed or clarified;
  4. your relationship to the work, so the correct action can be taken;
  5. a contact address for a response.

Factual corrections use the same route and are handled under the platform's editorial and corrections policy, which fixes the underlying knowledge rather than the individual reply.
