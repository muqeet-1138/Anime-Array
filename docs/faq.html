FAQ

The questions people actually ask

Straight answers, including the ones that are slightly awkward. If the answer you need is not here, the contact form is the fastest route to a real person.

The basics

What is Anime Array?
Anime Array is a free, independent, fan-built platform of AI tools for anime fans. It is a conversational platform rather than a streaming service and hosts no video of any kind. Its four products are character roleplay, the Otaku AI assistant, AniPick AI recommendations and Vision AI image generation.

Cost and accounts

Is Anime Array free?
Yes. Every core feature is free and no account is required. There is no subscription and no premium tier. The platform is funded entirely by clearly labelled advertising, and user data is never sold.
Do I need an account to use it?
No. Character chat, Otaku AI in both modes, all eight of its tools, AniPick recommendations and Vision AI generation all work without registering. An account is only needed to save chat history, and nothing important sits behind it. You can use every core feature without ever providing an email address.
Are there usage limits?
Limits exist where they are needed to keep abuse down. Nobody is asked to pay to get past one, and there is no paid tier that unlocks more.
Does it work with ads blocked?
Yes. Every core function described on this site works with advertising blocked. If you block every ad on Anime Array, you lose nothing that matters.

Content and copyright

Does Anime Array host anime or stream video?
No. Anime Array hosts no episodes, films, subtitles or other media files, and does not mirror or link for download any protected material. It is a discovery and conversation platform, not a broadcaster. There are no fake play buttons or download buttons anywhere on the platform, because those are the oldest tricks in the book and this site does not use them.
Is Anime Array affiliated with any anime studio or publisher?
No. Anime Array is an independent fan project with no endorsement, sponsorship, licence or approval from any studio, publisher, licensor, broadcaster or rights holder. Nobody has reviewed or approved the characters. Character and series names are used only to identify the source material the characters are inspired by, and all such names remain the property of their respective owners. More detail is on the policies page.
Are the characters real people?
No. They are AI simulations inspired by anime characters. They are not the real individuals, they are not the voice actors, and they do not speak on anyone's behalf. The platform does not use voice cloning: no character's dialogue is spoken by a real person in real time, and no voice actor's performance is reproduced.
Do character replies always match canon?
No, and this is deliberate. Character replies are original conversational responses generated in a character's voice — they are not quotations from any script, manga panel or dub. Where a character is given a situation the source material never covers, the reply is reasoned from that character's temperament rather than looked up. If a reply is treated as a quotation or a factual claim about the source material, it is being misread.
Is the content suitable for younger readers?
Some of the source series feature violence, horror and strong language, and characters may discuss mature themes. Anime Array does not add a filter layer to soften source material, because softening it would misrepresent the characters being simulated. Use the tools accordingly.

The products in detail

Which characters can I chat with?
Twelve are currently available: Gojo Satoru, Ryomen Sukuna, Naruto Uzumaki, Itachi Uchiha, Madara Uchiha, Obito Uchiha, Monkey D. Luffy, Roronoa Zoro, Son Goku, Prince Vegeta, Eren Yeager and Levi Ackerman — drawn from Jujutsu Kaisen, Naruto, Dragon Ball, One Piece and Attack on Titan. Each has a distinct voice and temperament, and more are added based on reader requests.
What is Otaku AI?
Otaku AI is the conversational anime assistant, available at otaku.animearray.site. It runs in two modes: Standard Brain for direct, knowledgeable answers about series, characters, lore and the platform, and Character RP for answers given in an anime character's voice. It includes eight built-in tools — Recommend Anime, Currently Airing, Mood Match, Anime Search, Character Creator, Anime Quiz, Power Scaling and Philosophy. Full detail is on the products page.
What is AniPick AI?
AniPick AI is the recommendation engine at animearray.site/anipick. It builds a shortlist from what you watched, your all-time favourites and the titles you dropped, rather than returning the same commercially popular list to every visitor. The titles you abandoned are weighted heavily, because they are the clearest signal available about taste.
What is Vision AI?
Vision AI is the anime image generator at animearray.site/vision-ai, used for character art, wallpapers and creative work. Generated images are original outputs and are not reproductions of any existing animation, manga panel, photograph or artwork. Requests to reproduce protected material or real people are refused.
Do the characters remember previous conversations?
Within a conversation, yes. Across conversations, saved history is what lets a thread be picked up later, and that requires a signed-in account. Sharing and exporting a conversation work without one.
Can I share a conversation?
Yes. Conversations can be shared as text, exported as a file, or turned into a shareable image card.

Trust, privacy and funding

How does Anime Array make money?
Advertising, and nothing else. Every advertisement is labelled as such and the funding model is stated plainly rather than buried. There is no subscription, no premium tier, no paid unlock on a core feature and no sale of personal data to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
Does Anime Array use my data?
Personal data is never sold to anyone. Accounts are optional, so no email address is needed for any core feature. Third-party services are called from the server, so integration credentials and identifiers are not exposed in page source. Analytics are used to understand aggregate usage rather than to build a profile of individuals, and site administrators are excluded from reporting so internal traffic does not distort the figures. The full position is in the Privacy Policy.
Is Anime Array accessible with a screen reader or keyboard?
Yes. Semantic HTML, keyboard operability for every interactive control, visible focus indicators that do not rely on colour alone, WCAG AA contrast against actual rendered backgrounds, labels on every form field, live-region announcements for streaming responses and honoured reduced-motion preferences are treated as requirements rather than enhancements. None of it is conditional on having an account. Full detail is on the about page.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Every product is built to be usable on a throttled mobile connection, and that is checked before a feature ships. Character replies begin arriving immediately and render progressively rather than appearing all at once after the full response completes.
What languages is it available in?
English currently, with more planned based on where readers actually are. Machine translation is not used as a substitute for a native speaker.

Reporting and contact

A character or fact is wrong. How do I report it?
Corrections are the highest-priority kind of report, and no technical skill is needed to make one. Send the character or page, the question asked and the incorrect answer through the contact form or email contact@animearray.site. The underlying knowledge is corrected, not just the reply. The editorial and corrections policy explains the process.
Can I request a new character?
Yes. Requests are collected through the AI Hub, and the roster follows what readers actually ask for rather than a fixed release schedule.
Can I report an accessibility barrier?
Please do, through the contact form. Accessibility reports are treated as defects rather than as suggestions, and fixing them takes priority over new features.
How do I report a copyright concern?
Through the contact form, ideally with the specific work, the URL on Anime Array, and what you would like removed. Copyright concerns are prioritised above everything else in the queue.
How do I report a security vulnerability?
Privately, not as a public issue. The reporting route is documented in SECURITY.md in the repository, and genuine reports are acknowledged and fixed.
The answer I needed is not here.
That is a gap, and it is worth telling Anime Array about through the contact form. The FAQ is written from the questions people actually ask rather than the ones that would be convenient to answer.
Still stuck?

Use the contact form or email contact@animearray.site. Messages are read and answered by a person.
